Ensulizole

By Angela Lee, Founder · ARW House · Updated October 8, 2026

The short answer

Ensulizole: up to 4% in the US; up to 8% in the EU; up to 4% in Australia.

Worth knowing

FDA safety review

This is one of 12 sunscreen active ingredients the FDA has proposed are not GRASE (generally recognized as safe and effective) because the public record does not contain sufficient data to support a positive determination. The FDA is asking for safety data on them. It is still listed in the current monograph, with the US limit shown above.

Source

Babies younger than 6 months

The FDA says sunscreen is not usually the answer for infants. Its advice: keep your baby in the shade as much as possible, cover skin with clothing and a hat, and talk to your pediatrician before using any sunscreen on your baby.

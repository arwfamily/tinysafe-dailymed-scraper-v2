SPF boosters in baby sunscreens, including mineral ones

By Angela Lee, Founder · ARW House · Updated October 8, 2026

142of 390

Each door is one baby or kids formula. Filled: contains at least one of the six booster ingredients.

142 of the 390 baby and kids sunscreen formulas listed in the FDA's DailyMed label database contain a synthetic ingredient that its manufacturer sells to boost SPF or stabilize UV filters, or that the EU regulates as a UV filter, listed under Inactive ingredients. That includes 79 of the 204 mineral formulas, whose only active ingredients are zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide.

Registry dataReview fileMethod

Not a violation: none of these ingredients is an FDA sunscreen active, so they belong with the inactive ingredients, as the rules require. The finding is about how labels work, not about any brand.

DailyMed lists drug labels submitted to the FDA, including labels for products made in US facilities for other markets. A listing is not proof that a product is on US shelves today.

butyloctyl salicylate10868Hallstar, a manufacturer, says that at 5% it is responsible for no more than 2 SPF units on its own, that a larger SPF boost can be seen with other filters, and that it can help prevent the breakdown of certain UV filters. Source
ethylhexyl methoxycrylene3022Hallstar sells it as SolaStay S1, a photostabilizer, and says it "photostabilizes and improves mineral filter efficacy". Source
polyester-8225Hallstar sells it as Polycrylene, a sunscreen photostabilizer, and lists "SPF Enhancement" as a feature. Source
tridecyl salicylate105Vantage sells it as JEECHEM TDS, "designed for use as a sunscreen booster", and says it can be combined with titanium dioxide or zinc oxide. Source
ethyl ferulate65Sold as CaribSun UV, which its supplier describes as having "antioxidant, free radical scavenging and UV adsorption properties". Source
polysilicone-1532A UV filter in the EU, allowed up to 10% under Annex VI of the EU Cosmetics Regulation. It is not an FDA sunscreen active. Source

One formula can contain several; 36 contain two or more. Counted in inactive ingredients as filed with the FDA plus the printed Drug Facts ingredient list.

What this means

A mineral sunscreen's active ingredients are zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide. On a US sunscreen, the Active ingredients section of the Drug Facts label lists only FDA sunscreen actives. 21 CFR 201.66

Ingredients that help reach the labeled SPF but are not FDA sunscreen actives therefore appear under Inactive ingredients. This finding counts six of them, each with its manufacturer's or regulator's own description, in the full ingredient lists of baby and kids sunscreens. In mineral formulas they matter most, because parents reading only the Active ingredients section see zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide alone. 79 of the 204 mineral formulas contain at least one. It is about how labels work, not about any brand.

How we counted

Population: unique formulations of sunscreen labels that say baby or kids in the product name or on the front panel (FDA DailyMed; selected by rule from the label, with edge cases decided by hand and every decision recorded with its reason; a claim such as 'suitable for children' alone does not count; labels for another market and labels that do not meet US sunscreen limits as listed left out; a listing that bundles several products is split into its products). That is 390 unique formulations, of which 204 use only mineral actives (zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide). DailyMed snapshot 2026-10-08.

Questions

Does this mean these products break the law?

Not a violation: none of these ingredients is an FDA sunscreen active, so they belong with the inactive ingredients, as the rules require. The finding is about how labels work, not about any brand.

Is a mineral sunscreen with these ingredients still a mineral sunscreen?

Yes. Its active ingredients are only zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide. These six ingredients are not FDA sunscreen actives, so they appear under Inactive ingredients. Parents reading only the Active ingredients section will not see them there.

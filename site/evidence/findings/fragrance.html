Fragrance in baby and kids sunscreens

By Angela Lee, Founder · ARW House · Updated October 8, 2026

117of 390

Each door is one baby or kids formula. Filled: lists fragrance or parfum.

117 of the 390 baby and kids sunscreen formulas listed in the FDA's DailyMed label database list fragrance (or parfum) as an ingredient. For children with eczema, the American Academy of Dermatology advises a sunscreen that is fragrance-free.

AAD guidanceRegistry dataReview fileMethod

Not a violation: fragrance is allowed in US sunscreens. The finding counts what the ingredient lists say; it is not about any brand.

DailyMed lists drug labels submitted to the FDA, including labels for products made in US facilities for other markets. A listing is not proof that a product is on US shelves today.

What this means

For children with eczema, the American Academy of Dermatology advises a sunscreen that is fragrance-free, and advises choosing fragrance-free rather than unscented products. AAD

This finding counts the baby and kids sunscreen labels whose ingredient list names fragrance, parfum or perfume. It is about what the labels say, not about any brand.

How we counted

Population: unique formulations of sunscreen labels that say baby or kids in the product name or on the front panel (FDA DailyMed; selected by rule from the label, with edge cases decided by hand and every decision recorded with its reason; a claim such as 'suitable for children' alone does not count; labels for another market and labels that do not meet US sunscreen limits as listed left out; a listing that bundles several products is split into its products). That is 390 unique formulations, of which 204 use only mineral actives (zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide). DailyMed snapshot 2026-10-08.

Questions

Is fragrance allowed in baby sunscreen?

Not a violation: fragrance is allowed in US sunscreens. The finding counts what the ingredient lists say; it is not about any brand.

What does the American Academy of Dermatology say?

For children with eczema, it advises a sunscreen that is fragrance-free, with titanium dioxide and/or zinc oxide, broad-spectrum protection and SPF 30 or higher, and it advises choosing fragrance-free rather than unscented products.

Why do some FDA records not show the fragrance?

In 102 of the 117 formulas, fragrance is printed on the label's Drug Facts but is missing from the ingredient list the labeler filed with the FDA. We count what either the printed label or the filing lists, and the printed label decides when the two disagree.

Do mineral sunscreens contain fragrance?

Some do: 28 of the 204 mineral-only baby and kids formulas list fragrance, against 89 of the 186 formulas that use other filters.

How were these products selected?

Population: unique formulations of sunscreen labels that say baby or kids in the product name or on the front panel (FDA DailyMed; selected by rule from the label, with edge cases decided by hand and every decision recorded with its reason; a claim such as 'suitable for children' alone does not count; labels for another market and labels that do not meet US sunscreen limits as listed left out; a listing that bundles several products is split into its products). That is 390 unique formulations, of which 204 use only mineral actives (zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide).

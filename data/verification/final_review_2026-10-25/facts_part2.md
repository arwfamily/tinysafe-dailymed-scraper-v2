## Missing counterpoints / out-of-date items
1. **retinyl_palmitate**: the sunscreen category question in F2 (0.05% body lotion vs 0.3% other). Also, EU compliance deadlines are 1 Nov 2025 (placing on market) and 1 May 2027 (making available). These are current as of Oct 2026 but worth stating.
2. **capb**: there is no other_side. A dermatologist would expect the CIR conclusion (safe when formulated to be non-sensitizing) and the point that sensitization is largely attributed to the impurities amidoamine and DMAPA. I did not verify these; source from CIR.
3. **lanolin**: CIR has assessed lanolin and its derivatives as safe in cosmetics. Consider adding it as a counterpoint (not verified here).
4. **drying_alcohol**: the topic must apply only to ethanol, alcohol denat., isopropyl and similar alcohols, not fatty alcohols such as cetyl, stearyl and cetearyl, which are emollients. Both AAP and AAD mean the short-chain kind. Check the ingredient matcher.
5. **propylene_glycol**: a 2026 Contact Dermatitis paper (Kanokrungsee, Dendooven, Aerts; doi 10.1111/cod.70211, "cosmetic skin sensitisers after all?") argues that PG allergy is under-detected at standard test concentrations. That cuts against O1's "uncommon" framing. Lead only; I did not review the full paper.
6. **talc**: add IARC's caveat that asbestos contamination and reporting bias could not be excluded (folded into the F3 correction).
7. **nano_minerals**: consider the OEHHA scope point (only airborne unbound respirable TiO2 is listed) so F3 does not imply that a TiO2 lotion is the listed form. Also, the SCCS opinions on nano ZnO/TiO2 exclude spray applications; F2 covers this indirectly.
8. **petrolatum_mineral_oil**: no other_side. The BfR finding applies only to highly refined (pharmaceutical-grade) oils, and US labels do not state the grade. Consider adding a not_known line.
9. **bha**: as of Oct 2026 the SCCS 0.07% opinion (Apr 2026) has not, as far as I found, become an Annex III restriction. Do not phrase it as law.
10. **food_allergen_skin**: the NIAID 2017 addendum is still the operative US guideline as far as I found. No newer NIAID version was located.
